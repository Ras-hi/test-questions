Q3. Maximum Valid Scratch Card Sequence
Sliding Window / Two Pointers
Problem statement
You are given two strings S1 and S2 of equal length, representing two scratch-card sequences.

You need to identify the longest continuous substring in which at most one position contains different characters between the two strings.

A position is considered:
- Matching if S1[i] == S2[i].
- Scratched if S1[i] != S2[i].
A valid continuous sequence can contain at most one scratched position.

Return the maximum length of such a continuous sequence.
