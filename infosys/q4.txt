Q4. Count the Number of Ways to Reach the Destination Using a K-Faced Die
Dynamic Programming
Problem statement
You are given a board containing N positions, numbered from 0 to N.

A person starts at position 0 and wants to reach position N.

A die has K faces, numbered from 1 to K. On each throw, the person moves forward by the number obtained on the die.

If the movement exceeds position N, the excess movement is rolled back to the beginning of the board, and the person continues from the beginning.

Find the number of possible ways to reach the destination.

For this reconstructed version, we assume that the board wraps around circularly, and we count ways to reach N for the first time. The exact original destination and counting rules still need confirmation.
