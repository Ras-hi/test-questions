Q2. Minimum Cost to Transfer Water Between Tanks
Prefix Sum / Greedy
Problem statement
There are N water tanks arranged in a straight line and connected by N-1 pipes.

Each tank has:
- A current water level.
- A target water level.
Each pipe has a transportation cost per unit of water transferred through it.

Water can be transferred between tanks through the connecting pipes. A tank can temporarily hold transferred water, but its final water level must equal its target level.

Your task is to calculate the minimum total cost required to make all tanks reach their target water levels.

If the total initial water is not equal to the total target water, return -1, because transferring water cannot change the total quantity.
